The Heart Behind the Flowers

Hi, I’m Tabitha.

I’m Tabitha, the founder and creative behind Soft Stem Co. I’ve always been someone who notices the little things. The detail that may seem insignificant to someone else is usually the very thing that catches my eye, and floral design has become a way for me to turn that attention into beautiful expressions.

When I create an arrangement, I’m drawn to the quirky details — the bend of a stem, an unexpected flower, the way something falls slightly imperfectly, or the one element that makes you look twice. I don’t believe everything beautiful has to be perfectly uniform. Sometimes, it’s the detail that feels a little different that gives something its character.

Soft Stem Co. is my expression of what I believe florals can be: luxurious without feeling untouchable, organic without feeling unfinished, and elegant without being predictable.

Whether it’s an intimate arrangement or a fabulous event, the goal is always to leave you with something you remember long after the flowers are gone.

Tabitha
