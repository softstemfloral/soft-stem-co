Pickup Policy

Fresh blooms, ready for you.

All Soft Stem Co. orders must be picked up during the scheduled pickup date and time confirmed with your order.

Pickup Location

Pickup is available at our designated Atlanta pickup location. The exact pickup address and instructions will be provided after your order is confirmed.

Scheduled Pickup Time

Please arrive as close to your scheduled pickup time as possible.

If you anticipate being late or are unable to make your scheduled time, please contact us as soon as possible. Alternate pickup arrangements are subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed.

After Pickup

Once an order has been picked up, the client assumes responsibility for the proper transportation, handling, and care of the florals.

Due to the delicate and perishable nature of fresh flowers, Soft Stem Co. cannot be responsible for damage or deterioration that occurs after pickup.

Missed Pickups

Orders that are not picked up as scheduled are not eligible for a refund.
