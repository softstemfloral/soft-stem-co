Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

Soft Stem Co. may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, contact information, order information, event details, and information submitted through contact forms or newsletter signup forms.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, communicate about orders, coordinate pickup, provide customer service, process requests, improve our website and services, and send marketing communications when you have chosen to subscribe.

Email Marketing

If you join the Soft Stem Co. email list, we may occasionally send seasonal newsletters, floral updates, special offers, promotions, collection announcements, and other business news.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in those communications.

Online Orders and Payments

If online ordering or payment processing is made available, payment information may be processed by a third-party payment provider. Soft Stem Co. does not intend to directly store full payment card information.

Sharing of Information

Soft Stem Co. does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate our website, process transactions, send email communications, or provide requested services.

Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand site usage, or improve the visitor experience. Third-party tools used on the site may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, or general site activity.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information submitted through our website. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request to update or correct personal information you have provided to Soft Stem Co. You may also unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

Updates to This Policy

Soft Stem Co. may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Soft Stem Co. through our Contact page.
