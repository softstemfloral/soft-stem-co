Refund & Cancellation

Because each Soft Stem Co. arrangement is thoughtfully designed and flowers are purchased specifically for each order, all sales are final once an order has been fulfilled.

Cancellations

Cancellations must be requested at least 72 hours before the scheduled pickup date to be eligible for a refund.

Cancellations made within 72 hours are non-refundable due to flowers, materials, and preparation already committed to the order.

If flowers or materials have already been specially ordered or purchased for your arrangement at the time of cancellation, those costs may be deducted from any eligible refund.

Floral Substitutions

While we make every effort to honor requested flowers and colors, floral availability varies by season and market.

When necessary, substitutions may be made with flowers of similar style, color, or value while preserving the overall look and feel of the design.

Order Concerns

If there is an issue with your order that is attributable to Soft Stem Co., please contact us within 24 hours of receiving your florals, along with photos of the arrangement, so we can evaluate the concern and determine an appropriate resolution.

Same-Day & Next-Day Orders

Same-day and next-day orders may be available on a limited basis and are not guaranteed. Availability depends on our current schedule, floral inventory, and the design requested.

Due to the short turnaround time, flower varieties and colors may be limited. When placing a same-day or next-day order, you are allowing Soft Stem Co. greater creative flexibility to design with the freshest flowers available while staying as close as possible to your requested style and color palette.

Same-day and next-day orders are final sale and cannot be canceled, changed, or refunded once confirmed.
